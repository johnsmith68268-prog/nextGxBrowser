Gaming or compiling in the background? Cap browser RAM, throttle CPU usage, limit network bandwidth, and kill memory-hungry tabs in one click — without losing your open workspace.

Privacy First

Block ads, trackers, and pre-roll clutter right out of the box. nextGx combines a fast native engine blocker, bundled AdGuard MV3 filters, and a YouTube player-response scriptlet that strips ad schedules without breaking video streams.
